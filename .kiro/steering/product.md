# Product Context

## Overview

A second-hand electronics trade-in platform focused on laptops, PCs, and MacBooks. The platform connects sellers who want to trade in their used devices, buyers who browse available stock, and admins who assess, test, grade, and price incoming products. The admin workflow is the primary focus — covering customer registration, product model selection, guided testing, defect grading (5 levels), photo capture with QR-based mobile helper, automated pricing, and stock management.

## Problem Statement

Trade-in businesses need a structured, repeatable process for assessing second-hand electronics. Manual assessment is inconsistent, slow, and error-prone. This platform standardizes the entire flow from customer intake to stock listing, ensuring fair pricing based on objective test results and defect grading.

## Target Users

- Admin Operation: Store staff who hands-on assess, test, photograph, and price trade-in products. The assessment workflow is built around their daily operations.
- Admin Manager: Supervisory staff who oversee operations, access the dashboard, manage approvals, and export reports.
- Seller: Device owners who want to check estimated trade-in value online before visiting the store.
- Buyer Storefront: In-store shoppers browsing available second-hand products at the physical location.
- Buyer Online: Online shoppers browsing available second-hand products via the web.

## Key Features

- Admin Assessment Workflow: 8-step process from customer registration through stock addition
- Product Testing: Model-specific test guides with structured result capture
- Defect Grading: Checklist-based defect identification with 5-level severity scale
- Photo Capture: Direct upload or QR code scan for mobile phone camera assist
- Pricing Engine: Automated price calculation based on model, test results, and defect grades
- Admin Dashboard: Overview of operations, stock levels, and activity metrics
- Excel Export: Export stock, assessment, and dashboard data to Excel
- Seller Price Check: Online price estimation using model, specs, and defect checkboxes
- Buyer Portal: Browse and view available second-hand products (storefront + online)
